A friend called me last week. Software engineer, decent salary but confused about finances.

"Abhilash, I want to buy a bike. ₹4 lakh. I already have EMIs running on my home loan and a personal loan. Can I afford this without wrecking my finances?"

He wasn't asking if he wanted it. He was asking if the math worked. He didn't know.

Most of us don't have a bad relationship with money. We have no snapshot of it. We know our salary. We don't know what it actually leaves us once every EMI and recurring cost is accounted for.

So I built Broke.

Enter your salary and existing EMIs, get a real answer on whether you can afford that bike, car, home, or even a career change. Not a vibe check. A calculation.

It also has:

Prepayment calculators showing exactly how much interest you save by paying early
	
Daily expense and investment logging, if you want the fuller picture

The name is the point. Before you buy the thing, know if you're actually broke or not.

Building this in public. Drop a comment if you want me to add something more in the app.
